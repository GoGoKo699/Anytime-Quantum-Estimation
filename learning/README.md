# Learn Anytime Quantum Estimation

A phase estimator can be excellent when its stopping budget is chosen in advance. This project asks what changes when the same run keeps publishing more precise answers and its entire published history must be trustworthy.

The selected educational anchor is **Andrew M. Childs, _Lecture Notes on Quantum Algorithms_, 17 April 2025**. Begin with the [anchor bridge](anchor-bridge.md), then follow the [30-milestone map](milestones.md). The bridge explains the query convention, gives a small interference example, and connects ordinary phase estimation to the all-time lower bound.

The map has 13 background milestones and 17 narrative milestones. The background connects phase queries, classical records, probability polynomials and Cantor geometry. The narrative assembles the propagation and first-error argument, then explains the matching schedule and its boundaries. These are understanding targets for any reader, rather than a record of anyone's assessments.

For the exact claims, go directly to [model and results](../science/model-and-results.md). For the proof, use [the scientific reading route](../science/README.md). The [finite verification](../verification/README.md) illustrates confidence allocation; it does not prove the lower theorem.
